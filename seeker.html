*~* My Awakening Story *~*


For 38 years, I lived what the world calls a "normal" life. I was Karen - wife to Dennis, mother to Tyler and Madison, dental hygienist at Sedona Family Dentistry. I drove a Dodge Caravan. I watched Jeopardy every night at 7:30. I thought this was all there was.

Then, in March of 1996, everything changed.

I was driving home from work through Oak Creek Canyon when I saw it - a brilliant silver light hovering over the red rocks. Time seemed to stop. In that moment, I heard a voice - not with my ears, but deep within my consciousness - saying: "Remember who you are. It is time to come home."

I pulled over and wept for an hour. I didn't know why. I just felt like I was being hugged by the entire universe.

Two weeks later, I found a flyer at the Whole Foods bulletin board for a talk by Master Zephron of the Brotherhood of the Celestial Light. Something told me I had to go. And when I walked into that community center and saw the gentle silver-haired man on stage speaking about the Galactic Federation and humanity's upcoming ascension... I knew. I finally knew why I had always felt like I didn't belong here. Why I always looked up at the stars and felt homesick.

I wasn't from here. None of us are.
