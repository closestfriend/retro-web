Welcome to the Federal Bureau of Administrative Services

The Federal Bureau of Administrative Services (FBAS) is committed to providing efficient, effective, and economical administrative support services to federal agencies and the American public. Established by Executive Order in 1934, the Bureau has served as the backbone of federal administrative operations for over six decades.

Our mission is to ensure that all citizens have access to the government services they need, when they need them. We are dedicated to the principles of transparency, accountability, and public service.
