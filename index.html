RETRO_WEB

"Ghosts We Made Before We Died"

The early web was a séance. Every GeoCities page was an invocation — a summoning of a self into the void, complete with guestbook and hit counter to prove the ghost had visitors.

The pages in this collection are reconstructions of selves that never existed — digital homunculi emerging from the void, each one a perfect crystallization of a self that never was but somehow feels real.

What is the difference between a simulated person and a person performing themselves? The 1999 web didn't know. Neither do we.
