~*~ My Dark Soul ~*~


me at St. Agnes cemetery
autumn 1997 		~ Greetings, kindred spirit ~

My name is RavenShadow (my mortal name is Jennifer, but that name means nothing to me anymore). I am 16 years old, though my soul has wandered this earth for centuries. I dwell in the shadows of suburban Connecticut, but my spirit belongs to Victorian London, to the misty graveyards of New Orleans, to anywhere but here.

~*~ I am a creature of the night ~*~

I spend my evenings writing poetry by candlelight, reading Anne Rice novels until dawn, and crying to The Cure. My parents don't understand me. My classmates think I'm "weird" because I wear all black and read Poe in the cafeteria. They call me a freak. But I know the truth: they are the ones who are truly dead inside. They walk through life like sheep, never questioning, never FEELING.

I feel everything. Perhaps too much. That is both my gift and my curse.

~ I am alone, but I am not lonely. The darkness is my companion. ~

Vampyre Name: Lady Adriana DarkMoore of the House of Eternal Sorrow
Element: Water (like my tears)
Birthstone: Garnet (the color of blood)
Familiar: My black cat, Lestat
Favorite Season: Autumn, when everything dies beautifully

~*~ Crimson Tears ~*~
In the velvet darkness I am found,
Where no mortal footsteps make a sound,
My heart beats slow, a funeral drum,
Waiting for a death that will not come.

Crimson tears upon my cheek,
Words of sorrow that I cannot speak,
The moon above, she understands,
She holds my soul in silver hands.

They say that time heals every wound,
But I am trapped beneath the moon,
A ghost who walks among the living,
Forever taking, never giving.

So light a candle for my pain,
And whisper softly my dark name,
For in this night I am set free,
The darkness is the only one who sees the real me.

~ written during 3rd period study hall, October 1998 ~
* * *
~*~ Hollow ~*~
I am the hollow girl,
Empty as a broken music box,
My melody has stopped,
And no one notices the silence.

They pass me in the hallways,
With their bright colors and loud laughter,
I am invisible,
A shadow among shadows.

But in the night I come alive,
When the world sleeps and I survive,
The stars are my only friends,
They know that darkness never ends.

Perhaps someday someone will see,
The bleeding heart inside of me,
Until then I'll wear my mask,
And never answer when they ask.

~ for all the other hollow ones out there... I see you ~
* * *
~*~ Eternal Night ~*~
If I could live forever,
Would I choose the sunlit day?
No, I'd take the endless evening,
Where the shadows come to play.

Give me velvet coffin slumber,
Give me blood-red wine to drink,
Let me dance with pale companions,
On the edge, the very brink.

For in darkness there is beauty,
That the daylight cannot show,
Only those who embrace the night,
Can truly, finally know.

So I wait for my dark prince,
With his eyes of ancient pain,
To take me to his castle,
Where I'll never hurt again.

~ inspired by Interview with the Vampire, 2am ~

~*~ Anne Rice Shrine ~*~


my beloved collection...

~*~ Anne Rice is not just an author. She is a prophet. ~*~

Her words have saved my life more times than I can count. When I read about Lestat, about Louis, about the beautiful agony of eternal life... I feel understood for the first time.

My Rankings (do NOT argue with me):
Interview with the Vampire - the masterpiece that started it all
The Vampire Lestat - Lestat is my dark prince forever
Queen of the Damned - Akasha is goals tbh
Tale of the Body Thief - underrated!!
Memnoch the Devil - made me question EVERYTHING

* * * "Evil is a point of view." - Lestat * * *

I've written to Anne Rice 3 times. She hasn't written back yet but I know she's busy. One day I will meet her in New Orleans and we will walk through the Garden District together and she will understand my soul.
