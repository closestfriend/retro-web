Korn is the reason I am who I am today. Jonathan Davis GETS IT. He knows what it's like to be in pain, to be different, to have everyone look at you like you're a freak. When I first heard "Blind" in 7th grade, my entire world changed. I finally understood that I wasn't alone.

  • Life Is Peachy (1996) - "A.D.I.D.A.S." is legendary

FAVORITE MEMBERS: Jonathan Davis (obviously), Munky, Head, Fieldy, David

🎤 CONCERT STORIES 🎤

★ OZZFEST '99 - THE GREATEST DAY OF MY LIFE ★

OK so this is the story of how I saw KORN at OZZFEST '99 and it literally changed my life forever.

My older cousin Derek (he's 21) took me and my friend Mike to Ozzfest at Devore. My mom didn't want me to go but my dad convinced her (thanks dad, you're alright sometimes). We left at like 6am to get there early.

The second stage had so many sick bands - Slipknot played and I had never seen them before and my MIND was BLOWN. These dudes in masks just DESTROYING everything. The mosh pit was INSANE. I got hit in the face like 3 times but I didn't even care.

But the MAIN EVENT was Korn. When they came out and the opening of "Blind" started playing... I literally cried. Not like sobbing or anything (I'm not a baby) but like tears just came out. I was in a crowd of like 20,000 people all screaming "ARE YOU READY" and I just felt like... THIS is where I belong. These are MY people.

Jonathan Davis came out in a kilt and played his bagpipes and it was the most beautiful thing I've ever seen. I bought a tour shirt (XL so it's nice and baggy) and I wear it like every week.

If you were at Ozzfest '99 email me!!! darksoUL666@hotmail.com

★ Family Values Tour '98 ★

Saw Korn, Limp Bizkit, Rammstein, Ice Cube, and Orgy. Fred Durst threw his hat into the crowd and this dude next to me caught it and I'm still jealous. Rammstein set stuff on FIRE. Ice Cube was cool but everyone was waiting for Korn obviously.
